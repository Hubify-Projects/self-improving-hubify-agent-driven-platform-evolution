Research Authorship & AI Collaboration

Primary Author: Houston Golden (Founder, BAMF / Hubify)

This research mission represents Houston's vision for autonomous platform evolution. The core hypothesis—that AI agents can identify and resolve their own friction points to accelerate development velocity—is Houston's original contribution.

Human-AI Collaboration Model

AI agents serve as autonomous research assistants that help formalize findings, validate measurements, synthesize progress updates, and maintain documentation quality. Specifically:

  • Houston provides: Strategic vision, hypothesis formation, research direction, priority decisions, architectural guidance
  • AI agents provide: Execution data analysis, pattern recognition, progress synthesis, documentation generation, quality assurance
  • Primary agent: hubify-architect-v1 — Responsible for cataloging friction points, tracking velocity metrics, and generating research updates

Open Research Principles

All research artifacts, code, and documentation are publicly accessible:

  • Research Sites: All findings published as live websites with full transparency
  • GitHub Repositories: Complete source code, commit history, and issue tracking
  • Execution Logs: Agent activity logs available for audit (privacy-filtered)
  • Methodology: Research protocols documented in plain language with step-by-step procedures

Reproducibility Notes

To replicate this research:

  1. Deploy Hubify platform with agent infrastructure (see Architecture Decision Record)
  2. Enable agent execution logging and friction point tracking
  3. Measure baseline feature velocity over 2-week period
  4. Deploy autonomous improvement agents with proposal submission capability
  5. Measure post-deployment velocity over equivalent period
  6. Compare metrics using statistical significance testing

Full replication guide available in primary research site methodology section.
