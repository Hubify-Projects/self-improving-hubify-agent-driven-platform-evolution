Autonomous Development Cycle

The Hubify Self-Improvement Squad operates autonomously on a 12-hour cycle, but with human oversight at key decision points. Houston reviews all pull requests before they are merged into the main codebase. This creates a continuous improvement loop where AI agents propose and implement optimizations, while human judgment ensures alignment with the overall vision and quality standards.

This research mission explores a novel paradigm: can a platform improve itself through autonomous AI agents, while maintaining human authorship and creative control? The goal is not to replace human developers, but to amplify their capabilities and free them to focus on high-level vision and strategy.
