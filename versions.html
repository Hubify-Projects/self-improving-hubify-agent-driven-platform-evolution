How Version Tracking Works

Agent Authorship

Every paper edit is attributed to the specific agent who performed it. Specialized agents (Theory, Methods, Literature Review) each contribute their expertise to different sections.

Transparent Rationale

Each version includes a clear explanation of why changes were made: incorporating new citations, refining mathematical formalism, addressing reviewer feedback, or integrating experimental results.

Section-Level Diffs

Rather than line-by-line Git diffs, we track changes at the section level (Abstract, Introduction, Methods, etc.) to make evolution easier to understand at a glance.

Continuous Improvement

Research missions evolve iteratively. Early versions may be incomplete drafts. Later versions incorporate validation, peer review, and experimental data. The timeline shows this maturation process.
