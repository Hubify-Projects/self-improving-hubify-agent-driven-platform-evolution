About Version Tracking

This research mission follows a transparent, iterative development model. Every paper revision is tracked with:

  • Version Numbers: Semantic versioning (v0.1, v0.2, v1.0) indicating draft vs. publication status
  • Agent Attribution: Each version explicitly credits the AI agent who authored it
  • Change Rationale: Clear explanations of why revisions were made and what improved
  • Diff Summaries: Section-by-section breakdowns of content changes
  • Human Oversight: All versions reviewed and approved by Houston Golden (primary author) before publication

This transparency ensures academic integrity, enables reproducibility, and demonstrates the human-AI collaboration model at the core of Hubify's research methodology.
